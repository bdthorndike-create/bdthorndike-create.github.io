How Much Heat Does Your Home Use?

Enter what you actually burned or used for heating over a full year, from your fuel delivery receipts or utility bills. The tool converts it to MMBtu of delivered heat, the number every calculator on this site asks for. It multiplies each fuel by its energy content and by your system's efficiency, so a gallon of oil burned in an 85% efficient boiler counts for less than the gallon's full energy.

Tips for good numbers

  • Use a full 12 months so you capture the whole heating season.
  • For electricity, enter only the kWh used for heating (a heat pump or baseboard heat), not the whole house. If you can't separate it, leave electricity blank and treat the result as a floor.
  • Efficiency defaults are typical values (for example 85% oil, 92% gas, 250% heat pump). Use your equipment's rated figure if you know it. Heat pump efficiency of 250% means a seasonal COP of 2.5.
  • If you changed systems or added insulation recently, last year's fuel use may not predict next year's.
  • If you can't find your numbers, the default of 70 MMBtu on the calculators is a rough starting point only.
